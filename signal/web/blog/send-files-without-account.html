Send files without an account

Every "free" transfer tool eventually asks for your email. Then comes the trial clock, the quota meter, the breach notification for an account you never wanted. No-signup sharing skips all of it: there is no identity to steal because there is no identity at all.

Why accounts exist (and why you don't need one)

Accounts exist to attach stored files to an owner: quota, billing, history, deletion. If nothing is ever stored, there is nothing to own — so the account form is pure friction. This app keeps room state in memory only, with no database of transfers and no history. No account is not a missing feature; it is the consequence of storing nothing.

The 60-second walkthrough

  1. Open the home page and drop files in.

    No landing form, no "continue with Google". The drop zone is the whole signup flow, and it takes one gesture.

  2. Copy the link, show the QR, or read the code.

    All three appear the instant your room is minted. Send whichever fits the conversation you're already in.

  3. Watch it land, then close the tab.

    Live progress, per-file checkmarks, and a completion animation. Close the tab and the room vanishes from memory — nothing to delete, no account to close.

Why this is the best setup for quick shares

Signups protect the provider's business model, not your transfer. For one-off sends between real humans, anonymous + encrypted + ephemeral beats "free account required" on every axis that matters: speed, privacy, and cleanup.

Questions, answered

Does the receiver need an account?

No. They open the link, press Download, and save. They never type a name, email, or password — on any door.

Is there a hidden limit for anonymous users?

No tiers, no trial. Anonymous is the only mode: unlimited size, unlimited transfers, same encryption for everyone.

How do I stop sharing?

Close the sender tab, or press Stop. The room dies instantly either way — that finality is the privacy guarantee. Details in the large-files guide.

Can I get a file while the sender is offline?

Not in P2P mode — both sides must be online together. For async pickup, pair this with cloud storage; compare in P2P vs cloud.
